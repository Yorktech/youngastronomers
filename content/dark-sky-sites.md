# Dark Sky Sites
## Find clearer skies for better stargazing

These locations are known for low light pollution and excellent night skies.

# UK Dark Sky Highlights

- Northumberland International Dark Sky Park
- Galloway Forest Park
- Exmoor Dark Sky Reserve
- Bannau Brycheiniog (Brecon Beacons)
- Yorkshire Dales National Park

***

# Night Sky Safety

- Go with an adult or group
- Carry a red-light torch
- Check weather and moon phase
- Respect local site rules
