## 🛰️ How We Study Deep Space

How do scientists know what stars are made of if we've never visited them? 

1. **Light & Spectroscopy:** By splitting starlight into a rainbow, astronomers read element "fingerprints" to determine what distant stars and nebulae are made of.
2. **Powerful Telescopes:** From optical reflectors on Earth to space observatories like James Webb, giant mirrors capture light that traveled for billions of years.
3. **Space Probes & Rovers:** Robotic explorers like *Perseverance* on Mars gather physical samples and transmit high-resolution images across millions of miles.
4. **Supercomputer Simulations:** Cosmic weather, galaxy collisions, and dark matter are modeled inside supercomputers before testing theories against real observations.

### 🚀 Fun Cosmic Facts

* **Stardust Humans:** Almost every atom in your body—iron in your blood, calcium in your bones—was forged inside the core of an exploding star billions of years ago!
* **Cosmic Speed Limit:** Nothing travels faster than light ($299,792\text{ km/s}$). Looking at the Sun means seeing it as it was 8 minutes ago!
* **Endless Worlds:** Astronomers estimate there are more stars in the observable universe than grains of sand on all the beaches of Earth combined.
