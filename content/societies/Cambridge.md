## Cambridge Young Astronomers

Astronomers aged 7 to 11 will love these Saturday morning sessions, held in the Hoyle Building at the Institute of Astronomy, Madingley Road, Cambridge, CB3 0HA.


A theme is given to each meeting, for example, galaxies, ancient astronomy, peculiar stars and the death of dinosaurs.


Meetings also include a short film in the lecture theatre and an activity session and run from 10:00 a.m. to noon on the last Saturday of every month.


The aim of Cambridge Young Astronomers is to make astronomy fun!


For further information, please email us at cambridgeastroassoc@gmail.com
