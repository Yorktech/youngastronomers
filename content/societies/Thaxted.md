## Thaxted Astronomical Society Young Astronomers Club


Do your children have an interest in astronomy and space? If so, they might like to meet up with other similarly-minded children learning about all aspects of the night sky including planets, stars and constellations, star clusters, nebulae and galaxies.


For further information please contact ThaxtedAS@BTInternet.com
