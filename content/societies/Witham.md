## North Essex Astronomical Society’s Young Astronomers Club (Witham, Essex)

North Essex Astronomical Society’s Young Astronomers Club is the perfect introduction for the budding astronomer!

Run by a friendly team of astronomers from the North Essex Astronomical Society – of which it is an integral part – you’ll learn about the night sky, how to use telescopes and participate in other great fun activities. On clear nights you will be able to look through their telescopes.

The club runs between 6.30pm and 7.30pm on the second Tuesday of each month (except for January and August) at Southview School, Conrad Road, Witham, CM8 2TA, and is for primary school-age children.

Each session costs £5 per child for non-members and £2 per child for members and includes a squash drink. Tea and coffee are available for adults for a small donation. Parents are required to stay and encouraged to join in. If space allows, our coordinator may accommodate children outside the age range; please get in touch with us to ask. Please note that prior booking for meetings is essential.

For further details please contact northessexastro@gmail.com
