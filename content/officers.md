## Officers

| Position | Officer | Duties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Secretary** | Brian Jones | Main point of contact between the public and Young Astronomers UK for general enquiries. |
| **Treasurer** | Pam Weller | Operates and maintains the Young Astronomers UK bank account; ensures payment of bills and society expenses; puts together the year end accounts. |
| **Advertising** | Jonathan Powell | First point of contact for companies and organisations wishing to advertise on the Young Astronomers UK website. Also seeks out potential advertisers. |
| **Publicity** | Brian Jones | Seeks out ways of publicising Young Astronomers UK and increasing public awareness of the organisation and its aims and objectives. |
| **Zoom Meeting Host** | Keith Maslin | Hosts Zoom meetings held on behalf of Young Astronomers UK. |
| **International Liaison** | Pam Ward | Facilitates contact and cooperation for mutual benefit between similarly-themed organisations across the world; seeks out support and resources from UK-based and overseas astronomy- and space-related agencies. |
| **Web Designer** | Steve Sawyer | Designs, operates, updates and maintains the Young Astronomers UK website. |
| **Social Media** | Steve Sawyer | Designs and creates social media postings for Young Astronomers UK and maintains a social media presence. |
| **Legacy Media** | *VACANT* | Publicising Young Astronomers UK through the traditional media of television, radio, newspapers, magazines and books. |
| **Fundraiser** | *VACANT* | Seeking out potential sources of revenue, either through grants, donations and so on or via fund raising activities instigated and run by Young Astronomers UK. |
| **Newsletter Editor** | *VACANT* | This role would entail the creation, compilation, frequency (perhaps two per year), targeting of readership (with assistance) and production of a Young Astronomers UK newsletter in electronic format; seeking out editorial and advertising copy for the newsletter |
